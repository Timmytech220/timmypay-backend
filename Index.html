Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains how TIMMYTECH DIGITAL SOLUTIONS handles refund requests relating to transactions made through TimmyPay.

We aim to ensure that transactions are processed accurately and that genuine transaction issues are investigated and resolved fairly.

1. Failed Transactions

If a transaction fails but your account or payment method has been debited, the transaction will be reviewed to determine its status.

Where a payment has been received but the requested service was not successfully delivered, the transaction may qualify for a refund or reversal after verification.

2. Pending Transactions

Some transactions may remain pending while TimmyPay or a third-party service provider confirms the transaction status.

Users should allow reasonable processing time before submitting a refund request for a transaction that is still pending.

3. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same transaction, please contact TimmyPay Support with the relevant transaction references.

Duplicate transactions will be investigated and, where confirmed, the applicable duplicate payment may be refunded or otherwise resolved.

4. Successfully Completed Transactions

Transactions that have been successfully completed and for which the requested service has been delivered are generally not eligible for cancellation or refund.

This may include successfully delivered airtime, data subscriptions, cable TV payments, or other digital services where the service has already been supplied.

5. Incorrect Transaction Details

Users are responsible for checking transaction details before confirming a payment.

Where a service has been successfully delivered to an incorrect account, phone number, smartcard number, or other details entered by the user, a refund may not be possible.

However, users are encouraged to contact Support immediately so that we can determine whether any corrective action is possible.

6. Third-Party Service Issues

Some TimmyPay transactions depend on third-party payment processors and service providers.

Where a transaction is affected by a third-party service failure, TimmyPay will make reasonable efforts to verify the transaction and resolve the issue based on the available transaction information.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or report a transaction problem, contact TimmyPay Support using the available support channels.

To help us investigate your request, please provide:

  • Your name or registered account details.
  • Transaction reference or receipt.
  • Transaction type.
  • Transaction date and amount.
  • A brief explanation of the issue.

8. Refund Verification

Refund requests may be reviewed against our transaction records and, where applicable, records from the relevant payment or service provider.

We may request additional information where necessary to verify a refund request and prevent fraudulent claims.

9. Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, the refund will be processed through the applicable payment channel or method, where possible.

The time required for a refund to reflect may depend on the payment provider, financial institution, or other third-party service involved.

10. Fraudulent or False Claims

Refund requests must contain accurate information. TimmyPay reserves the right to investigate and reject fraudulent, misleading, or intentionally false refund claims.

11. Contact & Support

If you believe you are entitled to a refund or have a problem with a transaction, please contact TimmyPay Support as soon as possible with the relevant transaction information.
